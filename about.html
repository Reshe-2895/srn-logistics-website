WHO WE ARE

Experience that
moves business forward.

Established in 1998, SRN Logistics is a family-led import and export business based in Chennai, India. Our work is shaped by decades of experience in air cargo operations and customs procedures.

Our founder began his career in 1987, building the industry knowledge and practical experience that continue to inform our business today. The company is now carried forward by his two sons, who are partners in the business.

We specialise in the import and export of precious metals, fine jewellery, precious stones and selected general merchandise. We remain committed to professional service, integrity and long-term relationships with our business partners.
